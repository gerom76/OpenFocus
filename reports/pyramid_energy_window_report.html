Every image below is a real fusion result, not an illustration. This report covers Pyramid, swept across energy window with levels=None held fixed. Window each band's focus energy is pooled over before the frames are compared. Small follows fine detail and speckles on grain; large decides regionally and rounds off narrow in-focus structures.
